*   **The Station of Play:** The royal lineage from which the Oberon was usurped (The PS5).
*   **The Brotherhood of the Discord:** The community of tech-necromancers resurrecting these boards.
*   **The High Priests:** The developers (e.g., **TuxThePenguin, Filippo**) who forge the BIOS and kernel patches.
*   **The Revisionary Spirits:** Specifically **Revision :C** of the silicon, the most resilient lineage documented in the late twenty-fifth year.

## III. The Sacred Rites of Resurrection (Procedures)
*   **The Rite of the Open Air:** The initial test boot upon the Altar of Cardboard.
*   **The Rite of Severance:** Mutilating the Aluminum Ribcage to allow for airflow.
*   **The Anointing:** Applying **PTM 7950** to the Mirror and **2.0mm pads** to the Monoliths.
*   **The Rite of Transference:** Flashing the modded BIOS (renamed to `Robin5.00`).
*   **The Rite of the Governor:** Installing the daemon to tame the feral voltage curves.
*   **The Ritual of Forgetting:** Clearing the CMOS via the **CLRCMOS1** shackle (Pins 2-3).
*   **The Rite of the 1750:** Accepting the native frequency of the Micron Monoliths. To push further is to tempt the Void.

## IV. The Mythology of the Damned (Curses & Phenomena)
*   **The Sanguine Tithe (The Blood Tax):** The mandatory physical injury sustained from the serrated heatsink.
*   **The Die-Shrink Exodus:** The prophecy of why the Oberon was cast out; Sony abandoned the 7nm forge for a smaller, colder brother (6nm), leaving these "orphans" to be salvaged.
*   **The Shackle of the Fourteen:** The native limit of the Micron Monoliths. Rated for **14Gbps (1750MHz)**, they resist the *Mirage of the 2000MHz* and will freeze if pushed too far.
*   **The Great Blinding:** The curse of **Linux Kernel 6.17.8** which plucks out the eye of the display.
*   **The Call of the Void (PXE Boot):** When **BIOS P5.00** ignores the SSD and screams into the Ethernet port.
*   **The Paradox of the 512:** Setting VRAM to **512MB** to unlock the magic of dynamic GTT allocation.
*   **The Hide-and-Seek of the Goblins:** The disappearance of M.2 screws or the **4 mini Phillips screws** that hold the I/O bracket.

*   **The Eight Nails:** The 8 Phillips screws holding the Backplate to the Heart.
