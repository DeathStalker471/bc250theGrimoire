**The Death Stalker Grimoires: Keter-Class Hardware Documentation**

**Description:**
Welcome to the archives of the **Brotherhood of the Discord**. This repository contains *The Iron Scripture of the Oberon*, a "quick start" guide for the BC-250 (PS5 Oberon/Cyan Skillfish) mining board, written not for the sane consumer, but for the desperate acolyte.

Forget dry technical manuals. We acknowledge the truth: repurposing e-waste is an act of necromancy.

**What is this?**
This is a fully functional technical guide disguised as a piece of grimdark/Lovecraftian horror fiction. It covers the complete setup of the BC-250, including:

*   **The Blood Tax:** Physical modification of the razor-sharp aluminum ribcage (heatsink) and how to survive the Pentad of Lacerations.
*   **The Rite of Severance:** The mandatory purging of factory sludge and the application of the Black Sacrament (PTM 7950) and the specific cushions of memory (2.0mm and 1.5mm thermal padding).
*   **The Transmutation of Mind:** Flashing the modded BIOS to break the Logic Shackles and unlock the forbidden 512MB VRAM allocation.
*   **The Fourteen-Fold Shackle:** Understanding the unyielding limits of the 14Gbps Micron Monoliths and the Law of the 1750.
*   **The Infusion of Soul:** Installing Bazzite or Fedora Linux to give the Prince a voice and a purpose.
*   **The Governor:** Taming the feral voltage curves of a GPU that was never meant to idle.

**Warning:**
The instructions within these grimoires are **real**, and the hardware is **dangerous**. The prose is gothic satire; the serrated aluminum fins, the conductive dust of the Motes of the Dead, and the high-current Font of Lightning are not. 

If you handle the Oberon with fear, the BIOS will remain shut. If you handle it with negligence, it will consume itself in fire.
